# First Hires

Design the founding team for a company with one founder. Default to fewer people, more ownership, and AI agents where they genuinely work.

Load `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/solo-founder-playbook/references/frameworks.md` (section 4) and `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/solo-founding-data/references/report-data.md` (Team).

## 1. Intake

Ask what's missing: stage, revenue, funding, runway, what the founder does well, what's eating their week, roles they're considering.

## 2. Work map

List the work the company needs in the next 6 months. Assign each item to one bucket:

- **Founder keeps** — sales until repeatable (Grinich, Ep. 13; Sonwalkar, Ep. 7), product taste and direction (Ep. 14), fundraising.
- **AI agent** — scoped like an employee: clear task, explicit authority, a human review point (Claire Vo, Ep. 20; Ben Cera, Ep. 1). Flag work where agents break at the last mile (Shivani Poddar, Ep. 34).
- **Contractor / work trial** — test before committing (Klein, Ep. 2; Du, Ep. 32).
- **Advisor / speed-dial bench** — expertise needed hours a month, not full time (Poddar, Ep. 34; Laberge, Ep. 27).
- **Full-time hire.**

## 3. Recommend the first one to three hires

For each: role, why now, what it unblocks, what to stop doing after they start.

Apply these checks:
- **Chief-of-staff trap.** If the founder wants a CoS to handle "everything," push back: hire operations and keep building (Paul Bakaus, Ep. 23).
- **Engineering first** is the norm for both solo and co-founded companies; operations is the next most common for solo (State of Solo Founding).
- **Multipliers, not delegators.** Look for people who make others better and prefer equity over cash (Sonwalkar, Ep. 7).
- **No cold hires; attract, don't chase** (Poddar, Ep. 34; Du, Ep. 32). Sources: open-source contributors (Shah, Ep. 15), high-agency people at big companies (Douglas, Ep. 10), DMs to people whose work you admire (Klein, Ep. 2).

## 4. Equity

- Median grants for the first five employees are similar across solo and co-founded companies (State of Solo Founding). Solo founders often go 2–5x median for early hires (field notes, same report). Be generous — no co-founder is taking 30–50% (Daniel Francis, Ep. 5; Chai Choudhary, Ep. 36).
- Large grants: consider 6-year vesting; keep the standard 1-year cliff.
- Founding-team framing: a small group with real ownership instead of co-founders (Francis Davidson, Ep. 16; Ali Ansari, Ep. 33). Path to elevate an exceptional hire later (Ep. 26).
- Advisors: templated equity, e.g., Founder Institute FAST agreement (Laberge, Ep. 27).

Give ranges with the reasoning; note that exact grants depend on stage, cash comp, and market, and that the founder should confirm terms with counsel.

## 5. Output

1. **Hire plan:** role → bucket → timing.
2. **First hire spec:** one paragraph and a three-line outreach message in the founder's voice.
3. **Trial plan:** paid project, duration, success criteria.
4. **Equity range** with reasoning.
5. **What the founder stops doing.**
